# Harmonies original illustration assets

2026-09-15. Generated with the built-in image_gen tool, then encoded as WebP at unchanged dimensions using the bundled Sharp utility. No project dependency was added. Reference card photographs and the user screenshot were used for layout/rule comparison; these assets are new illustrations.

- `animals.webp`: 1536×1024, eight columns × four rows, regular atlas; one species per basic card, ids 1–32 in reading order. Runtime uses CSS background positions, while card titles, habitat diagrams and points remain selectable/accessible UI.
- `landscape.webp`: 1536×1024, calm illustrated landscape for the lobby and tabletop.

## Prompts (built-in mode)

Atlas: Create one production sprite atlas for an illustrated nature board game. Exactly 8 columns and 4 rows, a perfectly regular edge-to-edge grid of 32 separate rectangular portrait paintings, with no gaps, borders, text, numbers, symbols, logos or UI. Overall image landscape 3:2, very high resolution. Each cell contains one recognizable animal in its habitat. Original sophisticated cut-paper and gouache editorial illustration, textured warm paper, geometric foliage, jewel teal, sage, gold and burnt sienna, luminous afternoon light. Row 1: crocodile in mangrove wetland, stingray underwater, newt underwater, sea otter in kelp, frog on lily pond, mallard at lakeside hut, flamingo in salt marsh, gecko on Mediterranean wall. Row 2: field mouse at wheat windmill, peacock in garden, squirrel in autumn trees, hedgehog in orchard, honeybee in wildflowers, brown bear in mountains, hare in meadow, blue and gold macaw in rainforest. Row 3: wild boar in forest, koala in eucalyptus, wolf in pine forest, kingfisher at river, penguin at ice shore, bat in twilight cave, fennec fox in desert canyon, snow monkey by hot spring. Row 4: bearded vulture over peaks, meerkat in savannah, crow at harvest farm, llama in Andes, white egret in marsh, raccoon by stream, ladybug on leaves, black cat in courtyard. Keep exact order and regular grid; no typography or card rules.

Landscape: Original production background illustration for a calm nature strategy board game. Wide landscape 3:2, hand-painted gouache and cut-paper storybook style, soft parchment texture. Winding turquoise stream through golden meadow, distant cream mountains, sage trees and tiny wildflowers, warm late afternoon light. Rich foliage frames the far margins. Keep the center 65 percent quiet light cream sandy meadow for interactive UI. Parchment, ochre, teal, forest green, terracotta. No text, logo, cards, tokens, hexagons, icons, UI or people.
